Allahu Akbar is not only "Allah is great." You've been saying "greater."
«اللهُ أكبر» ليست فقط «اللهُ عظيم». أنت تقول: «أكبر».

Greater than the fear that chases you, the person whose judgement weighs on you, the success that tempts you, and even the sin you think is bigger than His mercy. When your mind wanders in prayer, go back gently to the first words.

From My Prayer by Yasser Fares: https://yasserfares.com/books/my-prayer/?utm_source=youtube&utm_campaign=trackb
