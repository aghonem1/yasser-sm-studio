Allahu Akbar is not only "Allah is great." You've been saying "greater." Allahu Akbar meaning, explained from My Prayer.
«اللهُ أكبر» ليست فقط «اللهُ عظيم». أنت تقول: «أكبر».

Greater than what? The fear that chases you, the person whose judgement weighs on you, the success that tempts you, and even the sin you think is bigger than His mercy.

Nothing rivals Him.

And when your mind wanders in prayer, don't be harsh with yourself. Go back gently to the first words.

Send this to someone you pray with.

From My Prayer by Yasser Fares (link in bio).
