Allahu Akbar is not only "Allah is great." You've been saying "greater."
«اللهُ أكبر» ليست فقط «اللهُ عظيم».

Greater than your fear, the opinion of people, the success that tempts you, even the sin you think is bigger than His mercy. And when your mind wanders in prayer, go back gently to the first words.

From the book My Prayer by Yasser Fares, yasserfares.com
