Bismillah before Al-Fatihah: you don't start with your strength. Bismillah meaning, from My Prayer.
«بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ» قبل الفاتحة: أنت لا تبدأ بقوّتك.

What do you lean on most: your strength, your experience, or your worthiness? One word below.

You begin with the name of Allah. Not with your strength. Not with your experience or your worthiness. It frees you from a quiet illusion: that you are doing this on your own.

Mercy comes first. You are not walking up to a distant, unfeeling power, but to a Lord whose mercy embraces you.

Write بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ below if you'll say it slowly in your next prayer.
