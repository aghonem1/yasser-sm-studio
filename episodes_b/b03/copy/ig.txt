Alhamdulillah isn't only "thank you." You didn't make yourself, Allah did. Alhamdulillah meaning, from My Prayer.
«ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ» ليست «شكرًا» فقط. لم تصنع نفسك، الله صنعك.

Which do you credit to yourself: your job, your degree, or your success? One word below.

Praise is wider than thanks for one blessing. It is praise of Allah for His perfection, His acts, His wisdom and His gifts. Saying it returns every blessing to its true Source, and frees the heart from the illusion that you made yourself by yourself.

Lord of all worlds: you are not in an abandoned universe. You have a Lord who nurtured you through blessings, trials and guidance.

Write ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ below if you'll say it with one blessing in mind in your next prayer.
