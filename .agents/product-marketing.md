# Product Marketing Context: Yasser Fares

Document version: 1.0 (25 Nov 2026). Shared context for every marketing skill in `.claude/skills/`. Public repo: keep private figures (sales, list sizes, budgets) out of this file; they live in the Project docs.

## Product Overview
- **One line:** Yasser Fares is a Singapore-based author who returns to the words of the Qur'an and asks how they should shape the way we live. Tagline: "Return to the words. Live by their light."
- **What is offered:** five published books (ebook and paperback on yasserfares.com, also on Amazon); free reflections on the website (a new one most days) and a weekly reflection email; free live online sessions (webinars), the first on the seerah; talks; a paid seerah course planned for later.
- **Books:** The Mirror (The World Sees Islam Through Us) · Hold Firmly (Unite, Not Divide) · My Prayer (From the Call to a Life of Light) · The Evil I (The Self Between Delusion and the Truth) · The Language of Marital Repair (A Linguistic and Contextual Reading of Surah al-Nisa, 4:34-35). Do not state a count of books in bios or descriptions.
- **Shelf:** Islamic studies, Qur'an reflection (tafsir-informed, word-level), spiritual growth. English language.
- **Seller:** MANARAH PTE. LTD. (Singapore). Contact: hello@yasserfares.com.
- **Channels:** yasserfares.com · Instagram @yasserfares_ · Facebook yasser1fares · YouTube @yasser_fares · TikTok @yasser_fares · email list (MailerLite).

## Target Audience
- English-reading Muslims worldwide who want to understand what the Qur'an's words mean and how to live by them; starts from Singapore and the region, aims global.
- Also: people curious about Islam who meet it through Muslims' conduct (the theme of The Mirror); organisers of talks and study circles; partner organisations and mosques.
- Not B2B. No personas section.

## Problems & Pain Points
- Familiar words (kufr, niyyah, Allahu akbar, the nafs) are used daily but rarely examined; people inherit simplified or harsh readings.
- Prayer that feels mechanical; anxiety about "getting it right".
- Division among Muslims over differing opinions.
- Wanting depth without academic heaviness, in short daily doses.

## Competitive Landscape
Other English-language Islamic authors, teachers and reflection accounts, and short-form "Islamic reminder" content. Do not name or criticise other teachers or scholars in any content.

## Differentiation
- Starts from a single word or passage and its meaning, then draws out how to live it. Everything is sourced from the books; nothing is added.
- Calm, reflective tone; invites rather than warns.
- One cohesive message across all books, not centred on one title.

## Objections
- "Who is this author?" → show the work: reflections, free first chapters, talks, launch event.
- "Is this accurate?" → every piece cites its book and chapter; verses appear only as the book gives them.
- "Is this just selling?" → buying is the last step of a reading path, never the lead. Free value first (reflection, free chapter, free session).

## Switching Dynamics
Not applicable in the product sense. Habit to build: a short daily read or watch, then the weekly email, then a book or a live session.

## Customer Language
Use the readers' own plain words: prayer, intention, the heart, the self, mercy, unity, "what does X really mean?", "for whom am I standing?". Arabic terms are transliterated plainly and explained on first use. Avoid marketing vocabulary in public copy (funnel, offer, limited time, don't miss out).

## Brand Voice
- Plain, calm, warm, unhurried. Second person where natural. Short sentences.
- No hype, no urgency tricks, no scarcity, no guilt, no fear appeals, no clickbait that the content does not honour, no emoji in designed pieces.
- Hooks open on the reader or a real stake and must be literally true to the source.
- **No AI-voice line (Ahmed, 25 Nov 2026):** never write "The narration is an AI clone of Yasser's own voice." or any reworded AI-voice note, in captions, end cards, credits, emails, pages or ads.
- **Teaching, not selling (Ahmed, 25 Nov 2026):** free sessions are educational, religious events. Everything should read as "we are glad to teach you something". In videos: no buttons, pills, "link in bio", "save your seat" stickers or "register at the link" closes; the teacher invites, the date is said in his own voice, and the address is shown plainly.
- Visual house style: dark navy and gold, Cinzel caps, Cormorant italic, Spectral body. (The paper-collage look is for the long landscape teaser only.)

## Non-negotiable content rules (these override every skill)
1. Never invent Qur'anic text, verse numbers, hadith, scholarly positions, quotes or book references. Use only what the book or the published reflection contains.
2. Qur'anic Arabic only through `quran/verse.py`. No music under Qur'an recitation or text.
3. No faces, no AI people, no depictions of prophets.
4. Promotion never quotes or paraphrases the words of the Prophet ﷺ or his companions beyond what the source material states.
5. `RUNBOOK.md` and `research/learnings.md` in this repo come before any generic marketing advice. Marketing psychology is used to be clear and useful, never to pressure.
6. Paid courses are not led with; free sessions come first and each stands alone (not a "series").

## Proof Points
Published books (Amazon, Goodreads); book launch in Singapore, November 2026; reflections and talks pages on the website; free first chapter of each book by email.

## Goals
- Grow an audience that reads, watches and signs up: followers across the four social accounts, opt-in email subscribers, registrations for free live sessions.
- Consistent book sales directly through yasserfares.com.
- Low cost per sign-up; organic and partner-led first, paid ads small and tested.
- Every post points people who want more to the relevant book page: `https://yasserfares.com/books/<book-slug>/`.
