Can we judge what's in another person's heart? In Islam we deal with what people do, and leave the heart to God.

Every person has a face others can see, and a heart only God knows completely. We see one moment. God sees the whole road.

The truth doesn't reach everyone in the same shape. One person meets Islam through a Muslim whose honesty and mercy show its beauty. Another meets it only through anger, injustice or disturbing images. One reads the Qur'an in a language he understands. Another is handed fragments and poor translations.

So we can't tell how much light reached someone, or how much smoke stood in its way. Someone who seems far from faith today may be searching for it quietly. Someone we've counted as lost may, in God's sight, be moving towards the truth.
