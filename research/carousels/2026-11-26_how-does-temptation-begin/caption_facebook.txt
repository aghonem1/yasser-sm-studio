How does temptation begin? In Adam's story, Satan never told him to disobey Allah. He offered something better.

The second chapter of The Evil I goes back to Adam and the tree. Humanity didn't begin to fall when Adam stretched out his hand, the book says. It began a moment before, when the question inside his heart changed.

The whisper, as the Qur'an records it in Surah Ta-Ha, verse 120, offers eternity and then a kingdom. Everything in it turns on the self: what it will gain, possess and become. Temptation rarely begins by pulling us away from Allah. It begins by drawing our attention towards ourselves.

Wanting wasn't the sin. Desire is part of the nature Allah created within us. The danger came when desire grew louder than obedience, and that is where servitude is tested: when Allah's command asks us to rise above our wishes.
