Does wudu wash only your body? My Prayer follows the water from limb to limb and asks what each one has been doing.

After the call and the intention comes water. The third chapter of My Prayer calls water a visible mercy and treats each limb as a pause for reflection, so the water passes over the heart as it passes over the body.

The hands were created to work, build and give. A mouth that will read al-Fatihah shouldn't willingly backbite, lie or wound. A face that prostrates to its Lord shouldn't rise in arrogance over His servants. The arms should support truth rather than oppression. The head bows in worship to none but Allah. At the feet, the book asks about the roads they walk.

In this way, the book says, wudu gathers the whole person. The meaning of water is incomplete within us if nothing of it appears in our dealings.
