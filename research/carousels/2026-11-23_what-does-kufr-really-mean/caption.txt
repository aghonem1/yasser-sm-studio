What does kufr really mean in Islam? Its Arabic root means to cover, and only what is already there can be covered.

A word can be handled with care or thrown without thought. Kufr is one of the most thrown-about words we use.

In older Arabic a farmer could be called a kāfir. Why? He puts a seed in the ground and covers it with soil.

In Surah al-Baqarah (2:89) the truth came to a people, they recognised it, and then they rejected it. Rejection came after recognition.

So "non-Muslim" and "kāfir" aren't the same thing. One's an outward identity you can see. The other asks what actually reached a person, how it was presented, and whether they knowingly turned from it. You can't see that. None of us can.

None of this asks you to be less certain of Islam. You can believe it, explain it and invite others to it. What it takes away is the claim to see inside another person.
