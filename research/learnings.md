Current rules (confidence: low, n=4; revisit when n≥10):
1. Reach and resonance are different things. The real-life question reel reached most and was saved most; the Kufr surprise reel reached least but was shared ~4× more per viewer and converted most viewers to followers. Aim for both: a real-life stake in the hook, a surprising word fact in the body.
2. Length: no evidence either way yet. Keep 45–65 s as the idea needs; log length and compare with average watch time once available.
3. Conversion is the weak point: 10 follows from ~2,900 viewers (0.35%). Every reel ends with one reason to follow (the series promise: "a new word every day"), said and on screen.
4. Shares beat likes as the goal: the ask in caption and end card is "send this to someone who…", specific to the episode.
