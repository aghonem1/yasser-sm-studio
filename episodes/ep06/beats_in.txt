Don't stop at being fair.
The Qur'an asks you for more.
It's one word: birr. Generous kindness.
God does not forbid you from treating with kindness and justice
those who have not fought you because of your faith
or driven you from your homes.
Two words, side by side. Qist: justice. Birr: kindness.
Justice prevents us from harming another person.
Kindness moves us to help him.
Justice keeps the balance straight. Kindness fills life with warmth.
Here's the hard part.
If religious difference made kindness wrong,
the Qur'an would not have permitted birr.
So be just. Then go further: care, goodwill, active help.
Don't stop at fair. A new word, every day.
