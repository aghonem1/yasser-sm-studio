Kindness to neighbours of another faith? Quran 60:8 asks you for more than fair. Birr goes further.

Birr (بِرّ) means generous kindness. Qist means justice. The verse names both, and you're asked for both. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares puts it plainly: justice prevents us from harming another person. Kindness moves us to help him.

The hard part? If religious difference made kindness wrong, the Qur'an wouldn't have permitted birr. So be just. Then don't stop there.
