Kindness to neighbours of another faith? Quran 60:8 asks you for more than fair. Birr goes further.

Birr (بِرّ) means generous kindness. Qist means justice. The verse names both, and we're asked for both. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares writes that justice prevents us from harming another person, and kindness moves us to help him.

Does kindness to people of another faith weaken your faith? If religious difference made kindness wrong, the Qur'an wouldn't have permitted birr.
