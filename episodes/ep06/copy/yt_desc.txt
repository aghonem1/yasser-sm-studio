Kindness to neighbours of another faith? Quran 60:8 asks you for more than fair. Birr goes further.

Today's word from The Mirror by Yasser Fares is birr (بِرّ): generous kindness. The verse puts it beside qist, justice. Justice prevents us from harming another person. Kindness moves us to help him.

The hard part? If religious difference made kindness wrong, the Qur'an wouldn't have permitted birr. So be just, and then don't stop there.
