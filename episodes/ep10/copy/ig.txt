The word is ṣāliḥ (صَالِح, salih), righteous. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares traces its root to something sound, fit for the good purpose it exists for. Land is ṣāliḥah when it can produce life. A deed is ṣāliḥ when its effect is right and beneficial.

Quran 2:277 joins the two: those who believe and do righteous deeds. Faith is the root; righteous conduct is its fruit. A person may pray for a long time and still harm his neighbour. One sign of righteousness: people feel safe around you.
