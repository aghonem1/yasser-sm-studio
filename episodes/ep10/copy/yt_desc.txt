Today's word from The Mirror by Yasser Fares is ṣāliḥ (صَالِح): sound, fit for the good purpose it exists for. Land is ṣāliḥah when it can produce life; water is ṣāliḥ when it can be used safely; a deed is ṣāliḥ when its effect is right and beneficial. Quran 2:277 joins faith and righteous deeds: faith is the root, righteous conduct is its fruit. A person may pray for a long time and still harm his neighbour. One sign of righteousness: people feel safe around you.
