Hours of prayer, and still not righteous? Ask your neighbour.
One word: salih.
Its root, sad, lam, ha, means sound: fit for its good purpose.
Land is saliha when it can produce life.
Water is salih when it can be used safely.
A deed is salih when its effect is right and beneficial.
So it's not merely about appearance. It's about the effect.
God says: Surely, those who believe and do righteous deeds.
Faith is the root. Righteous conduct is its fruit.
Here's the hard part.
A person may pray for a long time, and still harm his neighbour.
Roots, but no fruit? The claim has no visible proof.
One sign of righteousness: your neighbour feels safe around you.
Prayer is a righteous deed. So is honesty at work.
Hours of prayer, and a neighbour who feels safe: that is salih.
