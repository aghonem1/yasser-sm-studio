Is going too far in religion more faith? Quran 4:171 says no. Ghuluw, religious excess, means crossing the line God set.

Ghuluw (غُلُوّ) means passing beyond the proper boundary. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares writes that the verse doesn't ask believers to weaken their commitment. It asks them not to exceed the limits God established.

Here's the hard part. Extremism means crossing the line while imagining you're protecting it. Advice turns into condemnation, and disagreement into hostility. Going beyond the limit is not greater obedience.
