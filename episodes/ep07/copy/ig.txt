Is going too far in religion more faith? Quran 4:171 says no. Ghuluw, religious excess, means crossing the line God set.

Ghuluw (غُلُوّ) means passing beyond the proper boundary. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares points out that the verse doesn't tell believers to weaken their commitment. It tells them not to exceed the limits God established.

The hard part? Extremism means crossing the line while imagining you're protecting it. Commitment becomes intolerance. Advice becomes condemnation. Disagreement becomes hostility.

Going beyond the limit is not greater obedience.
