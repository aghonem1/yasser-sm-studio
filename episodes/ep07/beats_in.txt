Don't confuse extreme with faithful.
Going too far is not more faith.
The Qur'an has a word for it: ghuluw.
Passing beyond the proper boundary.
God says: Do not go to excess in your religion.
Notice: He doesn't ask you to weaken your commitment.
He asks you not to cross the line.
So how does it start?
One piece of the faith,
held up as the whole.
Like one dark cloud,
and calling the whole sky darkness.
Here's the hard part.
He crosses the line while thinking he's protecting it.
Commitment becomes intolerance. Advice becomes condemnation.
Disagreement becomes hostility.
Going beyond the limit is not greater obedience.
Extreme isn't faithful. A new word, every day.
