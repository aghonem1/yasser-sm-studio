Not good. Better. That's the Qur'an's command to you.
One word: ahsan.
God says: Respond with what is better,
and the one between whom and you there was hostility may become like a close friend.
Notice: it doesn't just say, respond with something good.
It says: with what is better.
The answer most likely to heal, correct, and bring peace.
Think of a greeting.
Returning it is justice.
Returning it with something better is kindness.
Here's the hard part.
Not every answer you can give is one you ought to give.
You can win the argument, and lose the heart.
The point isn't to break the person in front of you.
It's to make the truth clearer.
Truth doesn't need insult to support it.
Not good. Better.
A new word, every day.
