Not good. Better. Quran 41:34 asks you to answer hostility with aḥsan: what is better, not just something good.

Aḥsan (أَحْسَن, ahsan) means better. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares notes that the verse doesn't merely say respond with something good. It says respond with what is better: the response most likely to heal, correct and bring peace.

Think of a greeting. Returning it is justice. Returning it with something better is kindness.

The hard part? Not every answer you can give is one you ought to give. You can win the argument and lose the heart. Truth doesn't need insult to support it.

Save this for the next argument you're tempted to win.
