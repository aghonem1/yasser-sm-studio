Not good. Better.

Quran 41:34 asks you to answer hostility with aḥsan, what is better, and The Mirror by Yasser Fares points out that the verse doesn't merely say respond with something good. It says respond with what is better: the response most likely to heal, correct and bring peace. Hard? Yes. You can win the argument and lose the heart.

Save this for the next argument you're tempted to win.
