Not good. Better. Quran 41:34 asks you to answer hostility with aḥsan: what is better, not just something good.

Today's word from The Mirror by Yasser Fares is aḥsan (أَحْسَن): better. The verse doesn't merely say respond with something good. It says respond with what is better: the response most likely to heal, correct and bring peace.

Think of a greeting. Returning it is justice. Returning it with something better is kindness. Not every answer you can give is one you ought to give. You can win the argument and lose the heart.

Verse: Surah Fussilat 41:34. English from The Mirror, Chapter Four.
