[curious] A man comes home from the cave of حِرَاء, trembling. He has just received the first words of revelation. The first person to hear it is his wife. And she reassures him.

[thoughtful] Her name is خَدِيجَة. Her answer was not a guess. She had known him for years: first as the man she trusted with her trade, then as her husband. So the question is simple. What had she seen?

[pause] She was not the only one who had been watching. When قُرَيْش rebuilt الكَعْبَة, the clans fell into dispute over who would place the Black Stone. They accepted his judgement. And the settlement let them all share the honour.

[serious] Then came حِرَاء. The account of عَائِشَة takes us through it, step by step: truthful dreams, a retreat, the opening words of سُورَةِ العَلَق, and the return home.

[warm] خَدِيجَة listens. And she answers from what she had witnessed in him. Then comes a second response, from وَرَقَة بْن نَوْفَل.

[curious] Who believed next? عَلِيّ. زَيْد بْن حَارِثَة. أَبُو بَكْر, and those he invited. The accounts differ on who came first among the men, and the reasons they differ are worth knowing.

[serious] And when the call went public, at الصَّفَا, someone else who had known him before revelation gave a very different answer. أَبُو لَهَب. Two people who knew him. Two opposite answers.
