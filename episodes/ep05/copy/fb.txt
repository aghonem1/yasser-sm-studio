No compulsion in religion (Quran 2:256). Force can make someone say the words, but it can't create genuine belief.

Ikrāh (إِكْرَاه) means compulsion. Force wins the tongue. Never the heart. In The Mirror, Yasser Fares writes that the heart is opened by truth, wisdom and sincere reflection, not by force.

Does that make belief and rejection equal before God? No. It means we choose, and we carry the responsibility for our choices (18:29).
