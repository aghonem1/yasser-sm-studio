No compulsion in religion (Quran 2:256). Force can make someone say the words. Not believe them.

Today's word from The Mirror by Yasser Fares is ikrāh (إِكْرَاه): compulsion. Force can't create genuine belief.

The heart isn't opened by force. It's opened by truth, wisdom and sincere reflection. The hard part? That doesn't make belief and rejection equal before God. It means we choose. And we carry the responsibility for what we choose (18:29).

Verses: Surah al-Baqarah 2:256, Surah al-Kahf 18:29. English from The Mirror, Chapter Two.
