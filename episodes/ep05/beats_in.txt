Stop forcing faith on people. Force wins the tongue, never the heart.
The Qur'an says it in one word: ikrah. Compulsion.
There is no compulsion in religion.
The right way has become clear from the wrong.
Force can make someone say the words.
But it cannot create genuine belief.
Say: The truth is from your Lord.
Whoever wishes may believe, and whoever wishes may reject.
Here's the hard part.
This doesn't make belief and rejection equal before God.
It means we choose, and we carry the responsibility.
The heart is not opened by force.
It is opened by truth, wisdom and sincere reflection.
So present the truth beautifully. Never force the heart.
