[serious] Stop forcing faith on people. Force wins the tongue, never the heart. [thoughtful] The Qur'an says it in one word: إِكْرَاه. Compulsion. There is no compulsion in religion. The right way has become clear from the wrong. Force can make someone say the words. But it cannot create genuine belief. Say: The truth is from your Lord. Whoever wishes may believe, and whoever wishes may reject. [lowering their voice] Here's the hard part. This doesn't make belief and rejection equal before God. It means we choose, and we carry the responsibility. [warm] The heart is not opened by force. It is opened by truth, wisdom and sincere reflection. So present the truth beautifully. Never force the heart. A new word, every day.
