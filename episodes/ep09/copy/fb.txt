Your parents are wrong. Would you still testify against them? Quran 4:135 asks us to stand for justice even against ourselves, our parents or our closest relatives.

The word is qisṭ (قِسْط, qist). The Mirror by Yasser Fares calls it a justice that gives every person his rightful due. It may mean admitting that someone close to us is wrong, and that an opponent is right.

Here's the hard part. Prejudice teaches us to overlook goodness in anyone who differs from us. Then religion stops being a scale of justice and becomes a lens that sees only the faults of others.

Justice is a scale that stays true, whether the person before it is loved or disliked.
