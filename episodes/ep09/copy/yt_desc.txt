Your parents are wrong. Would you still testify against them? Quran 4:135 asks you to stand for justice even against yourself, your parents or your closest relatives.

Today's word from The Mirror by Yasser Fares is qisṭ (قِسْط): a justice that gives every person his rightful due. It may mean admitting that someone close to you is wrong, and that an opponent is right. Prejudice teaches you to overlook goodness in anyone who differs from you, until religion stops being a scale of justice and becomes a lens that sees only the faults of others.

Justice is a scale that stays true, whether the person before it is loved or disliked.

Verse: Surah al-Nisa 4:135. English from The Mirror, Chapter Three.
