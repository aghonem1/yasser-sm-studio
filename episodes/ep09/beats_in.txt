Your parents are wrong. Would you still testify against them?
The Qur'an asks you to.
One word: qist.
God says: O you who believe, stand firmly for justice as witnesses for God,
even if it is against yourselves, your parents or your closest relatives.
Qist: a justice that gives every person his rightful due.
Justice may mean admitting that someone close to you is wrong,
and that an opponent is right.
A witness for God, not for group prejudice.
Here's the hard part.
Prejudice teaches you to overlook goodness in anyone who differs from you.
Then religion stops being a scale of justice,
and becomes a lens that sees only the faults of others.
Justice is a scale that stays true, whether the person before it is loved or disliked.
Your parents, or a stranger: the measure doesn't change.
One Qur'anic word a day. Follow so you don't miss tomorrow's.
