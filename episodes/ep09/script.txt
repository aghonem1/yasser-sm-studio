[curious] Your parents are wrong. Would you still testify against them? The Qur'an asks you to. One word: قِسْط. [thoughtful] God says: O you who believe, stand firmly for justice as witnesses for God, even if it is against yourselves, your parents or your closest relatives. قِسْط: a justice that gives every person his rightful due. Justice may mean admitting that someone close to you is wrong, and that an opponent is right. A witness for God, not for group prejudice. [lowering their voice] Here's the hard part.
